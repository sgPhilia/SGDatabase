## Reading path

1. **Essential, introductory:** V01-C01's primary-source selections (R-001, R-002) are used as historical/system overviews, not prerequisites for understanding the chapter.
2. **Essential, information theory:** Read Shannon (R-003) alongside V01-C02/C03; the paper is historically important but mathematically demanding.
3. **System examples:** Official PostgreSQL and SQLite sources (R-004–R-006) illustrate distinct product boundaries; they are not universal definitions.
4. **Later study:** R-007–R-010 are reserved for concurrency, execution, and recovery chapters.

This is an initial curated subset, not the complete core reading path or extended catalog. The original supplied PDF's domain-level source labels are not promoted to verified references without reconstructable bibliographic records.
